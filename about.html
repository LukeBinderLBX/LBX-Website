About LBX Technologies

Our engineers bring decades of combined manufacturing experience to designing and building automation solutions that improve productivity, safety and profitability for modern manufacturers and OEMs.

Mission

Engineers who show up on the plant floor.

LBX Technologies is an engineering firm built around on-site commissioning and project management. We serve manufacturers, OEMs and engineering firms, combining design-office discipline with hands-on field experience so projects finish on the plant floor, running and signed off.

We deliver from design through commissioning. Every project is held to rigorous quality standards — a reflection of our commitment to getting it right the first time and documenting it properly.

How we work

What you can expect from every engagement.

A project manager on site through construction, commissioning and startup, managing vendors and keeping the job on schedule.
