Our Services

LBX Technologies serves as an extension of your engineering team. We partner with engineering firms, OEMs & manufacturers to deliver on-site commissioning and project management.

Core services

On-site commissioning

We're on site from installation to handover, verifying every connection and bringing your equipment up to full production. We commission automation, process and mechanical systems, and represent OEMs and end users at startup.

  • On-site installation support
  • I/O verification
  • Startup & commissioning
  • Troubleshooting
  • Automation, process & mechanical
  • Process optimization
  • Representing OEMs & end users
  • Commissioning for cGMP and regulated manufacturing

On-site project management

On-site project management during construction, commissioning and startup. We focus on vendor management and keeping contractors, schedules and acceptance criteria aligned at the plant.

  • Vendor management
  • Contractor & trade coordination
  • Construction oversight
  • Commissioning & startup leadership
  • Schedule & scope control
  • Plant-level representation for OEMs & manufacturers

Supporting engineering services

Engineering & Validation

Automation development and engineering support, plus equipment qualification and validation for cGMP and regulated manufacturing when required.

  • Automation development & programming
  • Qualification planning and protocols
  • Turnover and readiness documentation

System Integration

Equipment, instruments and controls tied together into one working system.

  • Creating fully functional manufacturing systems
  • Integration of equipment, instrumentation & controls

Process & Mechanical Support

Finding the root cause and tuning the line for output and quality.

  • Throughput & product quality optimization
  • Process troubleshooting, inspections & audits
